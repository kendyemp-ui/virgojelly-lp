About Body After 40

Body After 40 is a small, independent page about what really happens inside the body after 40, explained in plain English.

Who we are

We're an independent affiliate publisher, not doctors and not the maker of any product. We summarize public research in plain English and point readers to options they can look into, always alongside a conversation with their own doctor.

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission from the seller at no extra cost to you. That's how we keep the page free. We only mention products sold with a money-back guarantee, and we don't invent reviews or testimonials.

Our sources

Statistics on the main page link to the published studies or summaries they come from. If you spot an error, tell us and we'll fix it.
