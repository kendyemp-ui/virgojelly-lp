Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

What we collect

Body After 40 does not ask for your name, email, or any personal information. The 60-second check runs entirely in your browser: your answers are not stored or sent anywhere.

Local storage

We save a few small values in your browser's local storage: which video or channel sent you here and whether you finished the check. This keeps the page consistent if you come back. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser data.

Hosting, logs and analytics

This site is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and performance, under its own privacy policy. We use Vercel Web Analytics, a cookie-free tool that reports aggregated visits and page interactions (for example, which package option you clicked or a button was clicked) without identifying individual visitors.

Affiliate links

When you click a link to a product, you leave this site and go to the manufacturer's site, where purchases are processed by ClickBank. Their privacy policies apply there. The link includes a tracking code (which channel or video sent you, and which package option you clicked) so the sale can be credited to us; it does not identify you personally to us.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.
